🛝 Chutes & Ladders: Consumer Math!

Climb the ladders, dodge the chutes, and practice CO 1–3!

🏁 Reach square 100 to win!

🎲 Game controls

🎲

How to play: Roll the die, then answer a Consumer Math question. A correct answer earns the full roll. An incorrect answer earns no movement. If you land at the bottom of a ladder, climb up! If you land at the top of a chute, slide down. You must roll the exact number to reach 100. Answers may include a dollar sign or spaces.

Objectives: CO 1 — deposits, withdrawals and balances; CO 2 — bank fees, alerts and overdrafts; CO 3 — slope, initial value and linear equations.
